# SUMAX Style — Presentations (.pptx)

## Format
- 16:9 widescreen (standard)
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Font: Arial only

## Slide Types

### Title Slide
- Logo top-left, large (280×38px equivalent)
- Gold line under logo
- Title: Black, bold, 36–44pt, left-aligned
- Subtitle: Gold #FAAC01, 20–24pt
- Date/Author: Gray #666666, bottom-left, 14pt

### Content Slide
- Logo top-left (180×25px equivalent), gold underline
- Slide title: Black, bold, 28–32pt
- Body text: Anthracite #393939, 18–20pt
- Bullets: Round black bullets (not gold)
- Max 5–7 points per slide
- Page number: bottom-right, Gray #666666, small

### Data Slide
- Chart primary color: Gold #FAAC01
- Chart secondary: Black #000000
- Additional series: #666666, #999999, #CCCCCC
- No 3D effects, no shadows
- Axis labels: Gray #666666, small
- Legend: Gray, outside or below chart

### Section Divider Slide
- Large title centered: Black, bold, 36pt
- Gold line below title
- Optional subtitle: Gold, 20pt
- Clean, lots of white space

### Closing Slide
- Logo centered, large
- Contact info below: Anthracite, 16pt
- Gold line above contact block
- "SUMAX – Reknova GmbH"
- Phoenixseestr. 20, 44263 Dortmund
- www.sumax.de

## Tables in Presentations
- Same as document tables: dark header (#1A1A1A), white text, light cells
- Borders: thin, #CCCCCC
- Font size: 14–16pt in presentations (larger than docs for readability)

## General Slide Rules
- Max 3 colors per slide (from the SUMAX palette)
- White space is intentional — don't fill every corner
- One idea per slide
- Images: full-bleed or contained with clear space
- No clip art, no Word Art, no gradient backgrounds
- Animations: subtle fade only, no fly-in/bounce/spin
