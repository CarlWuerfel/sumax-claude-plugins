# SUMAX Style — Presentations (.pptx)

## Format
- 16:9 widescreen
- Background: white (#FFFFFF); one dark slide type (#111111) for section dividers or a key statement is allowed
- Font: Arial (Inter is not available on every machine that opens the file). If the deck is exported as PDF only, Inter may be used.
- Logo: `assets/SUMAX_Ai_Driven_1.png` from this skill (1000×135 px, transparent); aspect ratio 7.4 : 1

## Slide Types

### Title Slide
- Logo top-left, large (≈ 280×38 px)
- Short gold bar (#FAAC01) above the title
- Label line above the title: small caps, bold, dark gold #8A6A12 ("ANGEBOT · OKTOBER 2026")
- Title: #111111, bold, 36–44 pt, left-aligned
- Subtitle: #5C5C5C, 20–24 pt
- Date / author: #5C5C5C, bottom-left, 14 pt

### Content Slide
- Logo top-left (≈ 180×25 px)
- Slide title: #111111, bold, 28–32 pt. The title states the message ("Ladezeit kostet 40 % der mobilen Besucher"), not the topic ("Ladezeit")
- Body text: #333333, 18–20 pt
- Lists: gold checkmarks or round ink bullets
- Max 5–6 points per slide
- Page number: bottom-right, #5C5C5C, 10–12 pt

### Key Figure Slide
- 2–4 large numbers, #111111, bold, 54–72 pt, with a short gray caption below each
- Thin ink line (2 pt) above each number
- Source of every number in small print at the bottom

### Data Slide
- Chart series: #FAAC01, then #111111, then #5C5C5C, #999999, #CCCCCC
- No 3D, no shadows, no gradient fills
- Axis labels #5C5C5C, small; grid lines #E6E6E6; legend below the chart
- Highlight the one value that matters in gold, everything else gray

### Section Divider Slide
- Background #111111, title white, bold, 36–40 pt
- Short gold bar above the title, optional label in gold (#FAAC01 is readable on dark)
- No logo (it is black and disappears on dark)

### Closing Slide
- Logo centered, large, on white
- Gold bar above the contact block
- Contact person with name, role, phone, email
- Sender: "SUMAX ist eine Marke der Reknova GmbH · Phoenixseestraße 20 · 44263 Dortmund · www.sumax.de"

## Tables
- Header row #111111 with white bold text, body rows white / #F7F7F7 alternating
- Lines #E6E6E6, numbers right-aligned
- Font size 14–16 pt

## General Rules
- Max 3 colors per slide from the palette
- Never bright gold text on white; gold text only as #8A6A12 on white or #FAAC01 on #111111
- White space is intentional, one idea per slide
- Images full-bleed or with clear space; real photos or custom visuals, no stock clichés, no clip art
- Animations: subtle fade only
